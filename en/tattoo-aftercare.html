Aftercare

Tattoo Aftercare

Keep this page after your session. Follow the option that matches the dressing your artist used, and follow any individual instructions your artist gave you.

Permanent care page · save or send this link
01 · Option 1

Healing film

Keep the film on for up to 5 days or as recommended by your artist. Remove it earlier if the edges begin to peel, water gets underneath, or the seal is broken. Remove the film under warm running water, slowly peeling it away from the skin. Wash the tattoo thoroughly with antibacterial soap. Pat dry with a clean paper towel and allow the skin to air dry. Apply a thin layer of Bepanthen Plus or the aftercare cream recommended by your artist. Continue aftercare 2-3 times daily until the tattoo is fully healed.

02 · Option 2

Disposable pad

Remove the first disposable pad after 3-6 hours. Wash the tattoo thoroughly with antibacterial soap. Pat dry with a clean paper towel. Apply a thin layer of aftercare cream. If necessary, use a fresh disposable pad only during the first 24 hours. Continue washing and moisturizing the tattoo 2-3 times daily until the tattoo is fully healed.

03

Important

  • Do not scratch, rub, or pick at the tattoo.
  • Do not steam or soak the tattoo.
  • Avoid swimming pools, saunas, steam rooms, and open water for at least 2 weeks.
  • Avoid direct sunlight for 2-4 weeks.
  • Wear clean, loose-fitting clothing.
  • Complete healing usually takes 2-4 weeks.
Studio 14

Questions during healing?

If something about your healing does not look or feel right, contact your artist before changing the aftercare routine.

Thank you for your trust. We wish you a smooth and comfortable healing process.
