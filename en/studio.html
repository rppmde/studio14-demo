STUDIO 14 / YEREVAN

The Studio

Studio 14 by Arthur is a tattoo and piercing studio in Yerevan. Founded by Arthur Barseghyan in 2025, it grew from years of shared work with local and international artists.

An independent space for tattooing, learning and exchange, connected to Armenia’s tattoo culture.

Inside Studio 14

A working studio in the city center. Space for a session, a conversation, or a quiet moment before you begin.

Our Story

From a room at home to a shared studio: a project shaped by artists working alongside one another.

2019

Home Studio

Arthur started in a home studio, where the first work and the idea for a professional space took shape.

01
2020

Chair at a Barbershop

He rented a room in a local barbershop and developed his practice there over the next two years.

02
2022

Underground Tattoo Studio

Underground was the first space equipped for several artists. Its name reflected both the basement location and the local underground artists it welcomed. Visiting artists helped connect the studio with an international community.

03
2025

Studio 14 by Arthur

Studio 14 continued that shared approach, bringing tattooing, piercing and education together. Courses are offered in Armenian, Russian and English.

04

Behind the session

The work starts with an idea, a conversation and preparation. The drawing, the placement and the setup all have their part.

A space with character

Art on the walls, books on the shelves and snapshots of people who have been here. Small details, collected over time.
