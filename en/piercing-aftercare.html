Aftercare

Piercing Aftercare

Save this page and use it throughout healing. Vahagn’s placement-specific instructions take priority if they differ from this general guide.

Permanent care page · save or send this link
01

Clean gently

Wash your hands before touching the piercing. Use packaged sterile saline labeled as a wound wash. The ingredients should be 0.9% sodium chloride (and sometimes purified water) without additives. Spray or rinse the piercing and dry gently with clean disposable gauze or paper products.

02

Leave the jewelry alone

Do not twist, spin, or rotate jewelry during cleaning. Keep the initial jewelry in place during healing unless Vahagn recommends a size, style, or material change. Downsizing and jewelry changes during healing should be done by a qualified piercer.

03

Avoid irritation

Avoid alcohol, hydrogen peroxide, iodine, harsh antibacterial products, ointments, and over-cleaning. Avoid friction, pressure, unnecessary movement, cosmetics or sprays around the piercing, and submerging it in pools, hot tubs, lakes, or other bodies of water while healing.

04

What can be normal

Early healing can include localized swelling, tenderness, bruising, discoloration, itching, and a small amount of whitish-yellow fluid that forms crust on the jewelry. A piercing can look healed on the outside while the inside is still fragile.

05

If you are worried

If you suspect an infection or a problem is getting worse instead of settling, contact Vahagn and seek medical advice. Do not remove jewelry on your own when infection is suspected unless a qualified professional tells you to do so.

Separate guide

Oral piercings

Lip, tongue, and other oral piercings need additional mouth-care instructions. Follow Vahagn’s instructions and the separate APP oral-aftercare guide.
