Hygiene & Safety

Piercing uses its own sterile setup, tools, jewelry and aftercare process. These steps explain what clients should expect around the procedure.

01

Sterile single-use needle

A brand-new sterile single-use piercing needle for each procedure.

02

Fresh gloves

Hand hygiene and fresh disposable medical gloves during setup and piercing.

03

Sterile implements

Reusable implements and jewelry are sterilized appropriately before use; single-use items stay single-use.

04

Surface disinfection

Before every session, work surfaces are cleaned and disinfected.

05

Needle disposal

Used needles are disposed of immediately in a dedicated container for safe needle disposal.

06

Aftercare & follow-up

Clients receive clear aftercare guidance and can contact the piercer during healing.
